RADIO MUSIC (UNOFFICIAL) - INSTALL GUIDE (macOS)
================================================

An unofficial plug-in inspired by Radio Music mk2 by Music Thing Modular
(designed by Tom Whitwell with Chris Johnson of Plus Minus Audio).
Not affiliated with or endorsed by Music Thing Modular.

This download contains:
  Radio Music Unofficial.component   (Audio Unit - use this one in Logic Pro)
  Radio Music Unofficial.vst3        (VST3 - for other hosts)
  LICENSE                            (GNU AGPL v3)

The plug-ins are universal (Apple silicon + Intel), for macOS 12 or newer.
They are signed ad hoc (not by Apple), so macOS flags them as "downloaded from
the internet". Step 3 removes that flag.

STEP 1 - Unzip
  Double-click RadioMusicUnofficial-macOS.zip in your Downloads folder.
  A folder called RadioMusicUnofficial appears.

STEP 2 - Copy the plug-ins into place
  1. In Finder, click the "Go" menu at the top of the screen, then "Go to Folder...".
  2. Type   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components   and press Return.
     (If the folder does not exist, make it: File > New Folder, name it Components.)
  3. Drag "Radio Music Unofficial.component" from the RadioMusicUnofficial folder into it.
  4. Optional, for VST3 hosts: Go > Go to Folder... > ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
     and drag "Radio Music Unofficial.vst3" into it.

STEP 3 - Remove the quarantine flag (Terminal)
  1. Open Terminal: press Command+Space, type Terminal, press Return.
  2. Paste this whole line and press Return (it prints nothing when it works):

     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Radio Music Unofficial.component"

  3. If you also installed the VST3, paste this and press Return:

     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Radio Music Unofficial.vst3"

STEP 4 - Check it passes Apple's Audio Unit validation (optional but recommended)
  In Terminal, paste and press Return:

     auval -v aumu Rmux Pdts

  Near the end you should see:  AU VALIDATION SUCCEEDED.
  If you see anything else, copy the whole Terminal output and send it back.

STEP 5 - Use it in Logic Pro
  1. Open Logic Pro and create a Software Instrument track.
  2. Click the instrument slot on the channel strip (it says "Inst 1" or is empty).
  3. Choose  AU Instruments > particledots > Radio Music Unofficial > Stereo.
  4. If it is not in the list: Logic Pro menu > Settings... > Plug-in Manager...,
     find "Radio Music Unofficial", tick it, then use "Reset & Rescan Selection".
     (Menu wording may differ slightly between Logic versions.)
  5. In the plug-in window click "CHOOSE FOLDER" and pick a folder containing
     .wav or .aif/.aiff files. Press Play in Logic, then drag along the tuning dial
     to change station. Hover over any control to see what it does.

UPDATING FROM AN EARLIER VERSION
  1. Quit Logic Pro completely.
  2. Repeat steps 2 and 3 above (copy over the old files, say Replace, then run the xattr lines).
  3. Open Logic Pro again.

UNINSTALL
  Delete the .component and .vst3 files from the folders in step 2.
